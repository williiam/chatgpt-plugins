Privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Billsmith does not collect your data. This page explains what that means in detail.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Billsmith plugin for ChatGPT and Codex and this website. Billsmith is published by williiam (github.com/williiam), referred to below as "the publisher".

Personal data collected by the plugin

None. Billsmith consists of instructions and a script that run inside your own ChatGPT or Codex session. It has no server, makes no network requests, uses no analytics and has no account system. The names, addresses, amounts, bank details and logos you provide are used only within your session to create your document. The publisher never receives them and cannot access them.

Purposes of use

Because the publisher receives no data from the plugin, the publisher does not use your data for any purpose.

Recipients

The publisher does not share, sell or transfer personal data, because it holds none. Your conversation and any files created in it are processed by OpenAI as the provider of ChatGPT and Codex, under OpenAI's privacy policy and your settings there.

Retention

The publisher retains nothing. Documents you create stay in your ChatGPT or Codex session, or on your own device, and are kept or deleted according to your OpenAI settings and your own choices.

This website and support requests

This website is static and sets no cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate the service, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. If you open a support issue on GitHub, whatever you post there is public and is handled under GitHub's terms. You can edit or delete your own issues and comments at any time.

Your controls

You decide what information to put into a document. You can remove the plugin at any time from the plugins page in ChatGPT or Codex, and manage or delete your conversations and files in your OpenAI settings.

Children

Billsmith is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/williiam/chatgpt-plugins/issues.

Billsmith is an independent plugin published by williiam. It is not made or endorsed by OpenAI.
