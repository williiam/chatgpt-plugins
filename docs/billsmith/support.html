Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests are welcome.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/williiam/chatgpt-plugins/issues. Issues are public, so leave out anything confidential and replace real names and amounts with examples.

Replies normally arrive within three working days.

What to include in a bug report

  • What you asked for, and what you expected
  • What happened instead, including any warning Billsmith reported
  • Whether you used ChatGPT or Codex

Common questions

Some characters show as question marks, or the result is an HTML file

The PDF can draw Latin-alphabet languages, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. For other scripts, such as Cyrillic, Arabic or Thai, Billsmith writes a print-ready HTML page. Open it in a browser and choose Print, then Save as PDF.

My logo was left out

Use a PNG or JPEG file. Transparent PNG files should be smaller than about 1200 by 1200 pixels.

Which tax rate should I charge?

Billsmith applies the rate you give it and cannot tell you which rate applies to your business. Please check with an accountant or your tax authority.

Can it send the invoice or take payment?

No. Billsmith only creates the file.
